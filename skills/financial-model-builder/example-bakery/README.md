# Example: a bakery in Kazan, Russia

A finished project produced with this skill. Everything inside is in Russian, because that is the language the client and the analyst worked in. The skill always works in the language of the user, so your own project will look like this in your language.

The client brief and the client's answers have been removed: they carried personal data.

## What the case was about

A full cycle bakery of 60 square metres in a residential district, bread, pastry and coffee to go, plus delivery through an aggregator. Horizon 36 months. The model showed an investment need of 4.09 million roubles against 3.0 million available, a payback at the sixteenth month of trading, and a funding gap of 1.13 million that appears a month before opening.

Two things in this case are worth copying into your own work. First, the interactive page was reconciled against the spreadsheet row by row across all 36 months, which is how four calculation errors were found in the spreadsheet itself. Second, the presentation ends on a screen of decisions with a price attached to each, which turns the meeting from a report into a decision.
