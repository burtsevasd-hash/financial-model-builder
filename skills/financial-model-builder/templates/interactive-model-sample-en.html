Input basis

Sources and assumptions

Client data

Monthly rent RUB 100,000; 60 m² premises; trading from 8:00 to 21:00 seven days a week; planned team; RUB 2 million in owner funding; renovation and equipment budget; average ticket RUB 250–300.

Market research

Average bakery ticket of RUB 259 from till data; seasonality from published research; 35% Yandex Eats commission from its commercial terms; electricity and utility tariffs from Tatarstan authorities; central-bank SME lending data; and an 8% microloan from the Tatarstan fund.

Recipe-card calculation

Ingredients and packaging equal 26.8% of revenue based on ten representative recipe cards and Kazan prices as of 8 October 2026. This includes packaging and 5% ingredient waste.

Regulation

The worked sample uses Russia's simplified income-minus-expenses tax at 15% under Tatarstan law, a 1% minimum tax, employer contributions of 30% up to 1.5 minimum wages and 7.6% above that level, fixed sole-trader contributions of RUB 61,154, and the RUB 20 million VAT threshold under Tax Code Article 145.

Analytical report

Should the neighbourhood bakery in Kazan open?

This worked sample models a 60 m² full-cycle bakery in a residential area of Kazan, selling bread, pastries and takeaway coffee, plus delivery through an aggregator. The 36-month model runs from November 2026 to October 2029, with opening in January 2027. It answers three questions: how much funding is needed, when the investment pays back, and how much profit the business can earn.

Most sensitive assumptions

  • Customer traffic. Daily transactions are the main driver and have no reliable public source. Published till data show a RUB 259 average bakery ticket in Russia, while bakery transactions fell 10% year on year in the first half of 2026. Measure footfall at the proposed address before signing the lease.
  • The 26.8% ingredient cost is based on representative recipes rather than the owner's actual recipes and assumes retail prices in the upper half of Kazan ranges. A 10% price reduction raises the ratio to 29.8%.
  • Ventilation. Without an existing food-service duct, a facade route may be required, needing building-owner approval and adding about six weeks.
  • Eligibility for the reduced 7.6% employer-contribution rate must be confirmed with the tax authority and requires at least 70% of prior-year revenue from production.

Actions before the decision

  • Count hourly transactions at three to five nearby bakeries on weekdays and Saturday.
  • Ask the landlord about the ventilation duct and obtain the previous tenant's December, January and February utility bills.
  • Obtain two renovation quotes and three ventilation quotes.
  • Confirm the 8% microloan terms and available amount with the Tatarstan Entrepreneurship Support Fund.
  • Register OKVED activity code 10.71 before 1 January 2027.
