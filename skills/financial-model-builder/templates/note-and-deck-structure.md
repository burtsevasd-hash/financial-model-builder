# Analytical note and presentation screens. Structure

Every figure is substituted from the page calculation and moves with the sliders. Plain language, short sentences, each carrying a figure, no judgement words and no advice to go ahead or not.

## The note

| Section | Contents |
|---|---|
| Header | Niche and city from the passport, the selected scenario and its three values |
| Opening paragraph | Format, horizon, opening date, and the client's three questions: how much to invest, when the money comes back, what the profit will be |
| How much money is needed | The investment need, what it is made of, own funds and borrowing, the gap or the headroom |
| Callout | If there is a gap: its size, the month it appears, and four ways to close it |
| When the investment comes back | Payback month of the model and month of trading, how it is calculated, the hardest month for cash |
| What the profit will be | Revenue and profit of the first full year, margin, profit over the horizon, closing cash, the three largest cost lines |
| What is most fragile here | Four assumptions with figures: the main driver, the unit cost, engineering or permitting risk, tax reliefs |
| What to do before deciding | Five actions with a verifiable result |
| Buttons | Download as a document, print or PDF |

## The presentation screens

Eight to ten screens. One idea per screen and no more than three figures. Speaker notes of two sentences under each.

| № | Screen | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title | Format, horizon, scenario and two of its figures |
| 2 | How much to invest | Four tiles: need, own funds, borrowing, gap |
| 3 | Cash on hand chart | Where and when the money runs out, the gap shaded, the minimum balance |
| 4 | When the money comes back | Payback month, month of trading, the cash trough |
| 5 | Cumulative cash flow chart | The trough and the crossing of zero |
| 6 | What the profit will be | Revenue, profit, margin, the share of each cost line |
| 7 | Margin of safety | For each key parameter, the point where payback is lost |
| 8 | Decisions before the start | Five decisions, each with its options and its price in money or in time |
| 9 | Risks | Where the model can be wrong, four points |
| 10 | Bottom line | Three figures and one question for the client |

Screen 8 is the centre of the meeting. The price of each decision is produced by running the model with that parameter changed, never estimated by eye.
