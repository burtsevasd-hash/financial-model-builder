# Support

Report reproducible problems at https://github.com/burtsevasd-hash/financial-model-builder/issues.

Include:

- the request with personal and company identifiers removed;
- whether the project language is English or Russian;
- the workflow stage;
- the expected and actual result;
- the smallest anonymized sample file needed to reproduce the problem.

Never publish client names, contact details, tax identifiers, bank details, credentials, contracts, or unredacted financial statements in a public issue.
